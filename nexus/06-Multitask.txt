MOD ADI (Name):
Multitask

KATEGORI ONERISI (Category):
Miscellaneous

KISA ACIKLAMA (Summary / Brief overview):
Do more than one thing at a time in Melvor Idle 2. Pick how many extra slots and play as usual: when you start a new action, the ones you were doing keep running. From two actions at once up to all of them, also offline. Nothing else to set up. A cheat.

[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is Multitask [b]0.7.0[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.7[/b] (the update of 25 November 2026) with Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0. Open a character, press [b]F5[/b] (or use the [b]Mods[/b] button in the top bar) and set [b]Extra slots[/b] with + and -.

[b]This is a cheat.[/b] The game is balanced around one action at a time; this mod lets two of them run together, or more, up to all of them. Use it if that is the game you want to play.

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]
There is one number to set and nothing to pick.
[list]
[*][b]1 extra slot:[/b] your last two actions run together.
[*][b]2 extra slots:[/b] your last three actions run together.
[*][b]All 15:[/b] every action you start keeps running. The game has 16 (15 skills plus combat); Farming and Ranching run on their own anyway.
[/list]
Fish, then start cooking: both run. Go mining, then start a fight: both run. Each at its normal speed, each with its normal rewards.

[size=5][b]You stay in charge, the way you already play[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Start something new:[/b] it joins. With no room left, an action whose materials ran out makes room first; otherwise the oldest drops out.
[*][b]Press the x next to it in the F5 window, or its own Stop button:[/b] that action is dropped. The others go on.
[*][b]Choose another rock, tree or recipe:[/b] that skill simply does the new thing. (A skill can only do one thing at a time.)
[*][b]Fewer slots:[/b] what no longer fits is stopped, the oldest first. With no extra slot (Off), the mod does nothing at all.
[*][b]Lock an action[/b] with the Lock button next to it: starting something new never pushes it out of its slot. New actions swap only among the slots that are not locked, so you can switch quickly without stopping it by mistake. One slot always stays free.
[/list]

[size=5][b]It looks after itself[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]It waits for materials.[/b] Cooking or smithing pauses when it runs out and goes on when there is more. It works in small piles (about ten at a time) so the game does not report "action stopped" for every single fish. Untick "Keep an action on the list when its materials run out" and it leaves the list instead.
[*][b]It keeps work done in steps.[/b] A heist's stage, a hunt's tracking, a potion's stirring and an Agility run's obstacle go on from where they were when you start something else (the game itself resets them whenever it stops an action). An Agility course with an entry fee is not paid twice.
[*][b]It keeps your "stop when".[/b] A crafting action's goal (make N, reach level N...) comes back with its progress when Multitask starts it again; once it is reached, the action stops for good.
[*][b]It does not walk back into a fight.[/b] A fight is dropped if your character dies.
[*][b]It works while you are away.[/b] Everything that was running when you closed the game goes on in the game's own offline progress.
[*][b]It never bypasses requirements.[/b]
[/list]

[size=5][b]Works with Action Queue[/b][/size]
With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/5]Action Queue[/url] 0.5.0 or newer, the queue runs as many of its own steps at the same time as there are places: one extra slot, two steps; two, three; and so on. Plan "100 cooked herring", press start, and it fishes and cooks at once. One button in the queue window switches that back to one step at a time, beside what you were doing. Locked actions keep their slots: the queue only uses the ones that are not locked.

[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]An action that runs out of materials while the game is closed stays stopped until you are back.
[*]Hunting needs a trap and Agility needs a path chosen on the skill's own page first.
[*]The game's bottom bar follows the page you have open: on the page of a skill that is running, it shows that skill (XP per hour, time to level, what it gathered). On any other page it shows your latest action. The full list is in the F5 window.
[*]All 16 skills the Action Queue knows are supported, including dungeons.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Melvor Mod Loader[/b] first (see Requirements).
[*]Open the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Extract [b]Multitask-0.6.1.zip[/b] there. The mod ends up in [b]mods\Multitask[/b].
[*]Start the game from Steam as usual.
[/list]
No installer, no .exe, no script. Nothing is written outside the game folder.

[size=5][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Press Off and delete the folder [b]mods\Multitask[/b]. Your save needs no clean-up: without the mod, extra actions simply stop the next time you start anything.
