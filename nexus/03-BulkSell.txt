MOD ADI (Name):
Bulk Sell - Bank Buttons

KATEGORI ONERISI (Category):
User interface

KISA ACIKLAMA (Summary / Brief overview, 228 karakter):
Adds buttons to the bank of Melvor Idle 2: a Bulk sell window with filters and "keep N of each", one-click Sell junk, Bury all and Open all. Uses the game's own prices and functions. Locked stacks are never touched. Not a cheat.

DEGISIKLIK LISTESI (Changelogs sekmesi):
0.4.0
- Auto-sell can sell gear for other combat styles: "Other styles" follows your One Style class (a Melee character sells Ranged and Magic gear), or tick Melee, Ranged or Magic yourself. Rings, amulets and capes never count. Asked for in play: a Melee character no longer has to look at Ranged and Magic Rares
- Auto-sell can sell Rares with 3 affixes (the ones with 4 stay) and every Magic and Rare item of the gear slots you pick
- Ready-made rules and a "Reset rules" button
- The affix buttons show their full text when you point at them, and how many items in the bank have each affix. A search box and groups (Attack, On hit, Defence, Gains, Active) make them easy to find
- "Show in the list" opens the items the rules match in the list, all marked, so you can keep some before you sell
- The preview turns orange when the rules match many Rare items; the last 30 auto-sales are listed with time and reason
- After a rule changes, only the next drops are judged by it

0.3.0
- Auto-sell (with One Style 0.4.0 or newer): rules sell new Magic and Rare items as they drop (only real new drops, never an item you take off or already own): every Magic, every Rare, below an item level, or with an affix you marked Sell; a Keep affix wins; locked, worn and Unique items are never sold
- Shift+click marks a range of rows
- New gear filters: slot, level ("Level 30 or lower") and, with the One Style mod, rarity (Magic, Rare, Unique...) and affix; "Clear filters"
- A Level column; rare items in their rarity's colour, their affixes when you point at the name
- The search also finds a rare item's base item and affixes
- Rare items are left off the junk list (each is one of a kind)
- The junk list and the auto-sell rules are kept per save (two characters with the same name no longer share them, renaming a character keeps them); lists from older versions are taken over once
- A list file that cannot be read is set aside and never written over

0.2.4
- Items can be taken off the junk list again: mark them and press "Remove marked from junk" (asked for on Nexus, thank you!). "Add marked to junk" adds without touching the rest
- Rows on your junk list say "junk" in the first column
- Adding or removing keeps the junk items that are not in the bank right now

0.2.3
- Its window can be opened from the loader's Mods menu, and its key (F7) changed or switched off there (loader 0.2.0)

0.2.2
- The kind filter "No skill uses it" is now called "Untagged": it shows items the bank has no kind for

0.2.1
- Leaves the game alone while it computes offline progress

0.2.0
- New: Bury all and Open all buttons

0.1.0

[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is Bulk Sell [b]0.4.0[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.7[/b] (the update of 25 November 2026) with Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0. Open the bank: the new buttons sit next to the search box. [b]F7[/b] opens the bulk sell window from anywhere.

[size=5][b]Bulk sell window[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Every sellable stack in one table: icon, amount, price each, what you would get.
[*]Click a row to mark it, Shift+click to mark a range. "All shown", "None" and "Invert shown" mark many at once.
[*][b]Auto-sell[/b] (with One Style): rules that sell new Magic and Rare items as they drop: every Magic, every Rare, Rares with only 3 affixes, below an item level, gear for the combat styles that are not your class, chosen gear slots, or an affix you do not want. A "Keep" affix saves an item from every rule except the style rule; locked, worn and Unique items are never sold. Ready-made rules set it up in one click, and "Show in the list" lets you look at what the rules would sell before anything goes.
[*]Filter by search text, by bank tab and by kind (Woodcutting, Smithing, ... or "Untagged").
[*][b]Gear filters:[/b] slot, level ("Level 30 or lower") and, with the One Style mod, rarity (Magic, Rare, Unique, Magic + Rare) and affix. Rare items show in their colour, their affixes when you point at the name, and the search finds affixes too ("lifesteal").
[*][b]Keep of each:[/b] leave a number of every marked item in the bank.
[*]Click a column title to sort, for example most valuable first.
[*]The bottom line always shows how many items go and for how much. Selling needs a second click.
[/list]

[size=5][b]One-click buttons in the bank[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Sell junk:[/b] mark the things you never want once and press "Add marked to junk"; from then on one button sells whatever of it is in the bank. Items on the list say "junk" in the table, and "Remove marked from junk" takes one off again.
[*][b]Bury all:[/b] buries every bone and other buriable item. Only shown when there is something to bury.
[*][b]Open all:[/b] opens every chest, bag and pack. Only shown when there is something to open.
[/list]
Each of these asks once before it acts: the button text turns into a question such as "Sell 229,484 for 1,360,052 Gold Pieces?" and only a second click does it.

[size=5][b]Your locks are respected[/b][/size]
A stack you have locked in the bank never appears in the list and is never sold, buried or opened.

The game already lets you select several stacks with Ctrl and Shift. This mod adds what that lacks: filters, "keep N of each", a junk list and the one-click buttons.
