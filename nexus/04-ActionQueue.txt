Chain actions in Melvor Idle 2: cut 1,000 logs, burn them, fish to level 30, then fight. Or name an item ("100 Steel Platebodies") and the planner works out every step. 16 skills, never bypasses requirements. Not a cheat.

[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is Action Queue [b]0.13.0[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.7[/b] (the update of 25 November 2026) with Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0. Open a character and press [b]F6[/b] (or use the [b]Mods[/b] button in the top bar, where the key can be changed). Drag the window by its title bar to put it where you like; a double-click there centres it again. Its right edge and bottom-right corner make it bigger.

[size=5][b]New in 0.13: run from a step, food, what comes next[/b][/size]
Select a step and press [b]Run from here[/b] (or right-click it): the queue goes on from that step. In lanes only that step's lane jumps, and a queue that is not running starts from there.

Equip now lists food too. Pick one and the skill that eats it (a fight, Thieving or Agility). A new goal, [b]the food runs out[/b], ends a step when that food is gone from the bank. Cook, put the food in, thieve until it runs out, start over.

Under the stop row, [b]When the queue ends, go on with[/b] starts one of your saved queues once this one is done or its stop is reached. The stop itself can now wait [b]when a round is over[/b], so the sale at the end of the round still happens.

The window can be made bigger by its right edge or bottom-right corner, and the Do and Until columns can be dragged wider in the list's title row.

All of this came from madmousepad (lmadrabbitl on GitHub), who built it for their own game and shared it. Thank you!

[size=5][b]New in 0.12: stop the whole queue, mastery tasks[/b][/size]
Under the buttons there is now [b]Stop the whole queue when[/b]: you own this many of an item, a skill reaches a level, or only this many of an item are left. Once that happens every step stops and the queue ends, even with "Start over at the end" on, in lanes and while the game is closed, and your idle action takes over. Farm a drop round after round (thieve, fish for food, cook it, start over) and the queue stops by itself when the drop is in your bank.

A step can now also wait for a Mastery Guild task: fish at Deep Blue until "Catch Deep Blue 8,000 times" is complete. Pick [b]a mastery task is complete[/b] and the list shows the tasks for your target first, with how far each has come.

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Pick what to do:[/b] choose a skill, then click the list and type to search (grouped like the skill page, with the game's pictures), or press "Use what I am doing now".
[*][b]Pick when it ends:[/b] when it stops by itself, when you own a number of an item, when you have made a number more of an item (whatever the bank already holds), when it has been done a number of times (every catch, hit, finished bar, lap or kill counts, like mastery), when it has used up a number of an item, when a skill reaches a level, after a number of minutes, when only a number of an item are left (fight until only 5 of your food are left), or when the food runs out (a fight, Thieving, Agility). For the item goal the item is filled in with what the action makes (Smithing: Gold Bar counts Gold Bars); you can still pick another one.
[*][b]Sacrificial Anvil per step:[/b] when the recipe has the game's anvil switch, tick the box next to it and that step smiths with the anvil; leave it unticked and it smiths without.
[*][b]Start the queue.[/b] One line at the top of the screen shows the current step and its progress.
[*][b]Start over at the end[/b] turns the queue into a loop.
[/list]

[size=5][b]16 skills[/b][/size]

[b]Slayer tasks as a step:[/b] "Slayer tasks of <master>" fights whatever that master's task is and walks to the new monster when a task is done. Unlike the game's own Auto Slayer switch it can have a goal, for example "until Slayer level 20", and then hand over to the next step.

[size=5][b]It plays fair and it plays safe[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Never bypasses requirements.[/b] A step you are not allowed to do yet, or have no materials for, is marked "skipped" with the reason, and the next one starts.
[*][b]You stay in charge.[/b] Start something else by hand and the queue pauses instead of fighting you. Press the game's Stop on a step and the queue moves on without it. "Remove all" also stops what the running step had started.
[*][b]Out of ammo ends the fight.[/b] When your bow has no arrows left (or your spell no runes), the fight step ends and the queue moves on, instead of standing in the fight while the monster hits you.
[*][b]Death pauses it.[/b] If your character dies the queue stops and does not walk back into the fight, even with "start over" switched on.
[*][b]Runs while the game is closed too.[/b] During offline progress the queue moves on to its next steps; a fight waits until the game is open again. After a restart it carries on where it was.
[*]Hunting uses the trap chosen on the Hunting page, or a trap from your bank when none is chosen. Agility needs a path chosen on the skill's own page first.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Melvor Mod Loader[/b] first (see Requirements).
[*]Open the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Extract the downloaded zip there. The mod ends up in [b]mods\ActionQueue[/b].
[*]Start the game from Steam as usual.
[/list]
No installer, no .exe, no script. Nothing is written outside the game folder.

[size=5][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [b]mods\ActionQueue[/b].

[size=5][b]Safe to download[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]One file: [b]ActionQueue.dll[/b]. No .exe, no .bat, no installer.
[*]Open source: read or build the code yourself on [url=https://github.com/falorfrozen-cmd/MelvorModLoader]GitHub[/url].
[*]None of the game's own files are changed or included.
[/list]
