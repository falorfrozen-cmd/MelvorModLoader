[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is One Style [b]0.4.0[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.7[/b] (the update of 25 November 2026) with Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0. Open a character, press the [b]Mods[/b] button in the top bar and pick [b]One Style[/b]. Click Melee, Ranged or Magic. [b]Off[/b] gives you the normal game back. The class is kept per character and can be changed at any time, for free.

[size=5][b]What your class opens[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Immune monsters:[/b] monsters immune to your style can be hit with it: Wind Drifter, the Amalgamations of the Sunken Tower and the Blood Sanctum dungeon.
[*][b]Quests:[/b] quest goals and monsters that ask for another style take yours. The archery targets of the first main quest can be shot with melee or magic.
[*][b]Level locks:[/b] requirements on another style's level read your class level: Battle Loadout, thieving distractions, the four combat constellations, item upgrades, Hunting by hand, rings and amulets. For Ranged also the crossbows and throwing hammers that ask for Strength.
[*][b]Dodging spells:[/b] for Melee and Ranged your Defence counts in full (the game normally uses Magic for 70% of it).
[*][b]Class tiers[/b] at class level 20, 40, 60, 80 and 99, each with a title and bonuses (per tier):
Melee +3% max hit, +4% minimum hit, -5% monster respawn time.
Ranged +8% chance to keep ammo, +4% accuracy, -2% attack time.
Magic +8% chance to keep runes, +4% accuracy, +3% max hit.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Equal footing[/b][/size]
Whoever you fight, no class is worse off than the others.
[list]
[*][b]Weak spot:[/b] every monster has a weakest style. Wind Elemental dodges magic 7 times better than ranged, Talm dodges ranged 10 times better than melee. With a class, your stances meet the monster's lowest evasion of all styles. Area and darkness effects still count.
[*][b]Combat triangle off:[/b] no style is good or bad against another, for any class.
[*][b]Elemental edge:[/b] Melee and Ranged get +15% max hit against the 135 monsters with an element, as a mage does with the right spell.
[*][b]Melee shares its XP:[/b] whatever Attack gains, Strength gains too, and the other way round. Every class has one skill to train. XP from quests, lamps and other mods is shared as well.
[*][b]Guild and Astrology equivalents:[/b] melee gets combat rewards from two guilds and two constellations. Ranged and Magic get the same from their own:
guild rank 3: +6% ranged strength / +6% magic damage
guild rank 4: -10% monster evasion and -10% monster armour
guild rank 5: +5% minimum hit and +15% power
Ranged, Syllia major 3: +4 Ranged levels
Syllia major 4 (Ranged) or Arachi major 4 (Magic): +15% accuracy
[*][b]Class identity:[/b] Melee 3% lifesteal. Ranged +5% critical chance and +25% critical damage. Magic +3 ward and 25% chance of double ward damage. Ranged and Magic carry +30 light (two-handed weapons leave no hand for a lantern).
[*][b]Pets:[/b] Fen and Bruno drop from Attack and Strength XP. For Ranged and Magic they drop from your class's XP.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Champions and Uniques[/b][/size]
Any of 179 monsters can come as a [b]Champion[/b] (blue outline) or, rarely, as a [b]Unique[/b] (orange, its own name, story and hand-made picture). Works with every class, and with Off. Switch it off on the Elites page. Elites only appear while you play, never while the game works out the time it was closed.
Under an elite's health bar you see CHAMPION or UNIQUE and a badge for every affix. The bar gets a frame, turns red while it is enraged, flashes green when it heals and shows its ward hits. The [b]Elites[/b] button next to "View Stats" in any monster list shows the monster's three versions side by side and what each one drops.
[list]
[*][b]How often:[/b] combat areas 6% Champion / 0.8% Unique, your slayer task 10% / 1.5%, dungeon waves 8% / 0.6%, a dungeon's last boss 10% / 4%. After 100 kills of a monster without its Unique the chance climbs until it is certain at 200 (60 and 120 before its first Unique).
[*][b]Champion:[/b] 1.35 to 3 times the hit points, +8 to 12% damage, 5% faster, one random affix (two from 1200 hit points).
[*][b]Unique:[/b] 1.7 to 6 times the hit points, +14 to 22% damage, 8% faster, its own two affixes, its own signature attack and sometimes a passive.
[*][b]Affixes:[/b] Hasted, Colossal (a quarter more hit points on top, so up to 3.75 and 7.5 times), Warded (2 to 6 hits of ward), Armoured, Vampiric (fades after 25 s), Enraging (below 40% hit points).
[*][b]Safety:[/b] a single hit is never more than 1.3 times the normal monster's, damage per second is capped, after 60 s an elite tires (+5% damage taken every 5 s), and while an elite lives you have no death penalty (setting).
[*][b]Rewards:[/b] Champion 2 and Unique 5 more rolls of the monster's own loot (gold too), +25% / +50% combat XP, Ember (the mod's currency: reroll a Magic or Rare item's numbers for 2 to 10 Ember in the bank, a small gamble: a window shows the item before and after, the new numbers stay). First kills give more; the first Unique of a monster gives its trophy.
[*][b]Trophies:[/b] 179 to collect. Each: +0.15% elite chance and +0.1% Ember. Milestones at 10, 25, 50, 100 and 179.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Rare items (new in 0.4)[/b][/size]
Monsters drop gear straight at its rarity, with random affixes, as in other hack and slash games. Into your bank, for every class.
[list]
[*][b]Magic[/b] (blue): 2 affixes. [b]Rare[/b] (yellow): 3, and 1 in 5 has 4. [b]Unique[/b] (orange): a fixed item of one monster's Unique. The colour is on the name, and as a frame in the bank and on worn slots.
[*][b]Drops:[/b] any monster 2% a kill (1% Magic, 1% Rare). Champion: 1 item (Magic 60%, Rare 33%, Rare with 4 affixes 7%). Unique: 2 items plus its own Unique item. Your slayer task and a dungeon's last boss: +5 item level and one more item from an elite. The base item is not from the monster's own drops: it is any gear near the monster's level (every slot as likely), so a Golbin can drop a rare Stone Sword but never a level 50 item. Gear that asks for no level counts as the level of its source (Hypnotic Amulet: 75). Caps on everything your rare items give together (attack interval -40%, lifesteal 10%, damage taken -20%, skill time -40%, skill XP +50% and more), and while you play nothing makes you attack faster than once every 0.6 s (Cheat Menu's Attack speed still works). Rare copies of one ring add all their affixes, but the ring's own stats count once. A button sells all your unlocked Magic items. Each monster's drop list shows the whole list ("Show possible items"), slot by slot, with each item's share of the rare items dropped.
[*][b]Tiers:[/b] the item level is the monster's combat level and sets the best tier: T5 below 15, T4 from 15, T3 from 35, T2 from 60, T1 from 85. T1 is the highest; the tiers' ranges never overlap. Each affix is one line on the item card with its tier; [b]hold Alt[/b] to see its range. The base item's own bonus is in a box of its own, so you always see what is rolled and what every item of that kind has.
[*][b]48 affixes:[/b] max hit, attack speed, accuracy, critical hits, lifesteal, poison / bleed / burn / stun / freeze / blind on hit, damage against elites, executing low enemies, armour, ward, hit points, protection, evasion, keeping runes, ammo, food and prayer points, double loot and gold, XP, faster respawns, more elites, more Ember and more. Caps keep the strongest ones in check.
[*][b]58 Unique items[/b] (weapons, armour, rings, amulets, two quivers that are never used up): 15% on every kill of a monster's Unique (no guarantee, they are worth farming). Each has its stats and a special power written on it: a free extra shot every 5th attack, a fatal blow survived once in 3 minutes, a sleeping enemy that takes +80%, elites twice as often with an extra affix and drop, and 54 more. The powers work while you play, not offline. Offline a Unique keeps its plain stats (a quiver that is never used up, lifesteal, accuracy), and the price a power takes (Twin Volley's -40% damage, Hollow Harvest's -35% hit points...) is not taken either. The powers that protect you rest offline too (Oathlocked Plate, Veil-Antler Reprieve, Cavern Bastion, Tollkeeper Greaves): do not leave a fight running that you only survive because of one of them.
[*][b]Safe in your save:[/b] rare items stay in the same bank tab and place (locked if you locked them), worn and in every loadout. Save copies and cloud saves carry them.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Six ring slots[/b][/size]
Every character gets six ring slots instead of one, whatever the class. Ring Slot 2 is open (the game has it behind Crafting 150), and four more sit on the equipment grid. Wear six different rings. They stay on in every loadout and after you close the game.

[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Weapons and armour of other styles keep their own requirements: a class stays a class.
[*]The class is not written into your save. It lives in onestyle.json next to the mod.
[*]Mastery Guild tasks that ask for another style are not changed yet.
[*][b]Removing the mod:[/b] just delete its folder. Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0 gives your rare items back as normal items (a Rare Bronze Sword comes back as a Bronze Sword) and the rings of Ring Slot 3 to 6 back into the bank, the next time you open each character. Nothing is lost, nothing is doubled. Removing the mod loader too? First press "Make all rare items normal" and "Put rings 2-6 in the bank" in the One Style window.
[/list]
