[*][b]Dungeon final boss pool:[/b] its rare drops roll from that dungeon's chest gear at the chest's own gear chance (e.g. Kraken's Domain 30%, Blood Sanctum 23%, Black Dragon's Lair 1%), with the chest's own weights.

[b]Base items, item level and tiers:[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Base item pool:[/b] not limited to the monster's own drops — picks any non-dungeon-only gear with level in [i][item level - 20, item level + 5][/i] (every slot equally likely; weapons match your class's style, or any style on Off). [*][b]Item level to best tier:[/b] 1-14 = T5, 15-34 = T4, 35-59 = T3, 60-84 = T2, 85+ = T1. Each affix rolls the best tier 50% of the time, one tier lower 35%, two tiers lower 15%. Tier ranges never overlap.
[*][b]Affix rules:[/b] at most 2 prefixes and 2 suffixes, never the same affix twice, at most one on-hit DoT (Poison / Bleed / Burn) and one on-hit stun (Stun / Freeze) per item.
[*][b]Requirements, prices and bank behaviour:[/b] rare items require their base item's skills at no less than 80% of item level (capped at 99); rings, amulets and capes use Hitpoints instead of a combat style so every class can wear them. Rare items count for set bonuses, cannot be upgraded, are excluded from the item log, show [b]Found in: Combat[/b], and sell for 1.5x (Magic), 2.5x (Rare) or 4x (4-affix Rare) base price (Uniques: 5x base, minimum 500 GP). Automatic placeholders do not leave empty ghosts behind when a rare item is sold. If your bank is full, drops wait and enter as soon as you free space.

[size=5][b]Built to survive game updates[/b][/size]
Melvor Idle 2 is in alpha and updates often. One Style is engineered so a game update does not eat your rare gear:
[list]
[*][b]Safe in your save:[/b] rare items stay in the same bank tab and slot (locked if you locked them), worn and in every loadout. Save copies and cloud saves carry them.
[*][b]Second copy outside the save:[/b] with every save (written only after the game confirms the save hit disk), One Style keeps a backup of your character's rare items — what they are, where each sits, how many — in [i]%AppData%\Games By Malcs\Melvor Idle 2\onestyle-items[/i]. [size=5][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Just delete the folder [b]mods\OneStyle[/b]. Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0 gives your rare items back as normal items (a Rare Bronze Sword comes back as a Bronze Sword) and puts the rings of Ring Slot 3 to 6 back into your bank, the next time you open each character. Ring Slot 2 is the game's own and keeps its ring. Nothing is lost, nothing is doubled: if your bank is full, remaining items wait and enter as you free slots.

[b]Removing the mod loader too?[/b] First open the One Style window on every character and press [b]"Make all rare items normal"[/b] (Elites page) and [b]"Put rings 2-6 in the bank"[/b] (Class page). If the window says any items are waiting for bank space, free some slots until they are in before removing anything.
