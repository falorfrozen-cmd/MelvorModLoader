DEGISIKLIK LISTESI (Changelogs sekmesi):
0.8.1
- New: "Infinite lanterns" on the Combat page. Lit lanterns are never used up in dark areas, one is enough
- New: "Ignore immunity" on the Combat page. Any attack style hits monsters that are immune to it (Wind Drifter, the Amalgamations); poison and bleeding stick to them too
- New: "No slayer area penalties" on the Combat page. Every slayer area loses its bad area effect (burn, less healing from food, quicksand...), as if you had bought each Area Effect Removal in the shop. Nothing is bought and no slayer coins are spent; switched off, the effects come back
- Fixed: "Slayer task: most kills" did not hold for extended tasks: they kept the master's own kills on top (with 25, up to 150 kills at the last masters). Now no task asks for more than the cap, at every master, extended or not
- Fixed: a new task could slip past the cap for a moment when its length happened to look like an extension
- Tasks rolled while the game was closed are capped too
- Fixed: One Style's rare and unique items showed up in "Give item" and the auto-sell list. A given one was broken (it vanished on reload) or a copy of a unique; they are left out now, they only come from monsters
- Checked on the game's 0.11.7 update with Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0

0.8.0
- New (asked for on Nexus, thank you!): "Ranching: animals never die" on the Skills page. While it is on, your ranch animals stop aging, also while the game is closed; switched off, they age on from where they stood
- New: "Infinite ammo" on the Combat page. Arrows, bolts, javelins, boomerangs and throwing hammers are never used up, one is enough. The ammo the death penalty takes is put back too
- "Complete all quests" (Tools page) now gives every quest's rewards, as if you had pressed Claim on each. Rewards that do not fit in the bank wait on the Quests page
- Your settings now also count for the time the game was closed: they are switched on before the game works out that time when you open a character

0.7.0
- New: "Slayer task: most kills" on the Combat page. No slayer task asks for more kills than this, for example 10. New and rerolled tasks get it at once; a task already running is shortened. "Extend task" still adds the master's usual kills. 0 = the game's own lengths

0.6.1
- New (asked for on Nexus): "Farming: normal pace" and "Ranching: normal pace" on the Speed page. Crops, the grain mill and animals keep the game's normal pace while Game speed runs everything else faster. Farming also ignores "Skill action speed". The skill's own speed on the Per skill page still counts
- Fixed: "Ranching speed" on the Per skill page did nothing, because the game never reads its product-time bonus. It now gives the animals extra ticks (while you play; not for offline progress)
- "Farming speed" now also speeds up the grain mill
- Its key (F8) can be changed or switched off in the loader's Mods menu (top bar button or F1, loader 0.2.0), so another mod can have F8

0.6.0
- New: the menu speaks seven languages: English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese), Español, Français, العربية (Arabic) and हिन्दी (Hindi). Traditional Chinese was first made by a player of the community, thank you!
- New: a language list at the top of the window, marked "Language". "Auto" follows the game's own language and shows which one it gives, e.g. "Auto (English)". Arabic mirrors the whole window (right to left). Names that come from the game (items, currencies) stay in the game's language

0.5.2
- New setting "Agility: no damage from a failed obstacle" (God mode only covers combat)
- New setting "Agility: keep the reward of a failed obstacle"
- New setting "Thieving: no damage when caught". "Never stunned" only stops the stun; getting caught also hurts, and this stops that.

0.5.1
- "Give item" and "Auto-sell from bank" have searchable lists of every item, grouped by the bank's own kinds (no longer cut off after 150)
- Auto-sell has its own item list and Add button instead of "Add the item picked above"

0.5.0
- Fixed: "Skill action speed" together with a skill's own speed could stop the game (everything froze until the setting was turned off). The two now multiply (x2 and x4 are x8) and stop at x20 together. The game's own speed bonuses are counted too, including those for a single animal or recipe (Hunting journal levels, for example).
- Fixed: a speed setting changed after planting did not count for crops already growing, so their old time stayed on the plot. Crops now get their new time at once.
- New page "Materials": mark a material (Coal, for example) as not needed and no recipe or item upgrade asks for it any more.
- New setting "Astrology: knowledge per study" (up to x1000).

0.4.2
- Fixed: with Game speed above 1x, "Loading Offline Progress" could take many minutes or hang. Offline progress is now left entirely to the game.

0.4.1
- Auto-sell and the loot filter leave locked bank stacks alone

0.4.0
- New look, loot filter, "All skills at once", settings apply at once, English only

[size=5][b]Start here[/b][/size]
This is Cheat Menu [b]0.8.1[/b], tested on game version [b]0.11.7[/b] (the update of 25 November 2026) with Melvor Mod Loader 0.3.0. Open a character and press [b]F8[/b] (or use the [b]Mods[/b] button in the top bar, where the key can be changed or switched off).

[b]Languages:[/b] English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese), Español, Français, العربية (Arabic) and हिन्दी (Hindi). Pick one in the list at the top of the window; "Auto" follows the game's own language. Traditional Chinese was first made by a player of the community.

Your settings are [b]not written into your save file[/b]. They live in a small file next to the mod and are applied again each time you open that character. Remove the mod and the character is back to normal. Only what the Tools page gives you (items, currency, levels) stays.

[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Melvor Mod Loader[/b] first (see Requirements).
[*]Open the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Extract [b]CheatMenu-0.8.1.zip[/b] there. The mod ends up in [b]mods\CheatMenu[/b].
[*]Start the game from Steam as usual.

[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Game speed makes the game run faster while you play. It does not change how fast the game computes offline progress.
[*]Ranching speed works with extra ticks for the animals (the game has no bonus that speeds Ranching up), so like Game speed it counts while you play, not for offline progress.
[*]A speed setting also counts for crops that are already growing: the plots show their new time at once.
[*]On the Materials page, Farming recipes follow after the character has been opened again.
[*]Changing a slayer task is never completely free: the game charges at least 1 coin.
[*]With double loot at 100%, bones more than double, because they also get the game's separate bone bonus.
[*]Melvor Idle 2 is in alpha. After a game update a setting can stop working until the mod is updated; it is then skipped and named in the log.
[*]This mod changes the balance of the game. Use it the way you enjoy playing.
[/list]
